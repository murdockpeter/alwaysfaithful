# Geography attribution and scale notice

The prototype geography resources mirror the current Sea of Uncertainty Luzon Strait visual dataset.

- Coastline: Natural Earth 1:10m land and minor islands, public domain, locally cropped and simplified. Not for navigation.
- Elevation/bathymetry: NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information, ETOPO 2022 15 Arc-Second Global Relief Model, DOI 10.25921/fd45-gt74. The packaged subset is sampled to two arc-minutes. United States government work; retain attribution. Not for navigation.

These inherited resources are suitable for a shared broad visual language and pipeline proof. Their packaged resolution is not sufficient for authoritative terrain, cover, movement, or line-of-sight decisions on a 250 m tactical hex map.
